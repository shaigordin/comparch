Assignment 4: Bronze Age Axes Outline Analysis

15% of final grade | Due: Week 9

Overview

Run a full EFA + PCA outline analysis on the Bronze Age flanged axe dataset, test for group differences, and produce a geographic visualization. Interpret the results in terms of Bronze Age craft networks.

Data

  • Images: data/flanged_axes/G3/ (30 axes) and data/flanged_axes/G4/ (35 axes)
  • Colab notebook: Week 9 notebook (link on Data page)

The images are .tif files showing single axe specimens against a white background. Your outline extraction code should work directly on these images.

Required Analysis Steps

Step 1: Outline extraction

Using the extract_outline() function from Module 8, process all axe images. Report: - How many axes were successfully processed? - Did any images fail? If so, why (look at the images)?

Step 2: EFA

Fit EFA with 12 harmonics, normalized. Produce a variance explained plot showing how many harmonics are needed to explain 99% of outline shape variation in your sample.

Step 3: PCA on EFA coefficients

Run PCA. Report variance explained by PC1, PC2, PC3. Produce: - Scatter plot color-coded by group (G3/G4) - Reconstructed outline shapes at −2 SD and +2 SD on PC1 and PC2

Step 4: Statistical test

Run the permutation MANOVA on the first 10 PC scores. Report: F, p, R².

Step 5: Geographic visualization

Using the provided metadata CSV (data/flanged_axes/axes_metadata.csv), plot specimen locations on a map colored by PC1 score.

Written Interpretation (600 words)

Your interpretation must address:

  1. Morphological separation: Do G3 and G4 separate in shape space? Describe the PCA plot. Is separation clean, partial, or absent?

  2. What drives the difference: Based on the reconstructed outline shapes, what is actually different about the axe shapes at opposite ends of PC1? Be specific: blade width? Proportion of butt to blade? Shape of flanges?

  3. Statistical support: Report and explain your F, p, and R². Does the statistical significance match what you see visually in the PCA plot?

  4. Geographic pattern: What do you see in the map? Does PC1 variation correlate with geography? Do specimens from the same region cluster together in shape space?

  5. Archaeological interpretation: Based on your results, what would you say about Bronze Age craft networks? Choose one of the following positions (or argue for a nuance):

    • G3 and G4 represent distinct production traditions with limited interaction
    • G3 and G4 represent a continuous temporal evolution of the same tradition
    • Shape variation is primarily geographic, not typological
    • Your data cannot distinguish between these models (explain why)

  6. One limitation: Identify one specific limitation of this analysis that could affect your conclusions, and suggest how future research could address it.

Map Requirements

The map must show: - Specimen locations as dots - Dots colored by PC1 score (use a diverging colormap, e.g., blue-to-red) - Group membership indicated (by symbol shape or border color) - A colorbar labeled “PC1 score” - Basic geographic reference (country borders or major rivers — even a hand-sketched map on paper, photographed, is acceptable)

Deliverables

  1. Colab notebook link (viewable)
  2. PDF report — 600-word interpretation + figures embedded:
    • Variance explained plot (scree plot)
    • PCA scatter plot
    • Reconstructed outline shapes (PC1 extremes minimum)
    • Statistical output
    • Map image

Grading

Criterion Points
All 5 analysis steps completed correctly 30
Reconstructed shapes produced and discussed 15
Map produced with correct coloring 10
Statistical output reported and explained 10
Archaeological interpretation is substantive 30
Writing quality 5
Total 100
